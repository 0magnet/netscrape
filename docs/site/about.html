What just happened

  1. The address went to the Go chrome, which called the page transport.
  2. The transport fetched it — here a plain same-origin fetch; in skywire's wasm visor the same seam dials the dmsg mesh instead.
  3. The HTML was rendered into a sandboxed srcdoc with a <base>, so its relative URLs still resolve.
  4. Stylesheets and images were requested by a shim inside the sandbox, relayed to the chrome by postMessage, and handed back inlined.
